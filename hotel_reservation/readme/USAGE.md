1. *Hotel > Reservations > Reservations*: create a reservation with the
   guest, the stay dates and the number of guests, then add lines with a
   room type and its rooms. Only rooms free for the whole stay are offered.
2. *Confirm* books the rooms; it fails when one of them is already booked
   by another reservation or a folio for those dates.
3. *Create Folio* turns the reservation into one folio with a room line
   per room. *Send Reservation Email* sends the confirmation; a reminder
   is sent automatically the day before the arrival.
4. *Hotel > Reservations > Reservation Summary* shows the rooms day by day
   (free, reserved, on a folio). Click a free day to book that room, or a
   booked day to open its reservation or folio.
5. *Hotel > Reports > Hotel Reservation Report* prints the check-in and
   check-out lists, the reservations of a period and the most used rooms.
