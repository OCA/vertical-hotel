Usage
=====

1. *Hotel > Reservations > Reservations*: create a reservation with the
   guest, the stay dates and the number of guests, then add lines with a
   room type and its rooms. Only rooms free for the whole stay are
   offered.
2. *Confirm* books the rooms; it fails when one of them is already
   booked by another reservation or a folio for those dates.
3. *Create Folio* turns the reservation into one folio with a room line
   per room. *Send Reservation Email* sends the confirmation; a reminder
   is sent automatically the day before the arrival.
4. *Hotel > Reservations > Reservation Summary* shows the rooms day by
   day (free, reserved, on a folio). Click a free day to book that room,
   or a booked day to open its reservation or folio.
5. *Hotel > Reports > Hotel Reservation Report* prints the check-in and
   check-out lists, the reservations of a period and the most used
   rooms.

Changelog
=========

20.0.1.0.0
----------

- Migration to Odoo 20.0: access rights in ``security/ir.access.csv``,
  no ``report_file`` on the reports, Owl 3 room summary. No longer
  depends on ``website_sale`` (it was only used by the demo pricelist).
- A reservation with several rooms now creates **one** folio with all
  its rooms (it created one folio per reservation line, billing rooms
  twice).
- Billable days follow the folio line rule: a 2-night stay checked in
  and out at the same time is 2 days, not 3.
- Draft and confirmed reservations can be cancelled from the form; a
  reservation with a folio cannot be cancelled (cancel the folio
  instead). Each button checks the state on the server.
- Rooms become occupied when the stay starts, not when the reservation
  is confirmed. The room status job only changes the rooms whose status
  changes and no longer fails when a room is on a reservation and a
  folio.
- Availability is the same everywhere: a room is taken by a confirmed
  reservation or a folio overlapping the stay; a stay ending when the
  next one starts does not block it. Confirming checks folios too, the
  room list of a reservation line only offers free rooms, and the folio
  room list leaves out the rooms of confirmed reservations.
- Reports: the period start was ignored and "From/To" printed empty; the
  Reservation List shows the stays overlapping the period, Max Room Used
  is sorted by use.
- New Reservation Summary: a free day opens a quick reservation for that
  room and day, a booked day opens its reservation or folio; also for
  hotel users. The grid shows at most 93 days.
- The 24 hour reminder email is sent (the scheduled job never found any
  reservation). Duplicating a reservation works again.
- Removed the unused "Make Folios" wizard.
- Upgrade: saved reservation summaries are deleted (the grid is
  computed).
