1. Configure the tables in *Hotel > Configuration > Tables* and the food
   items and their types in the Restaurant menu.
2. **Table booking**: *Hotel > Restaurant > Table Booking*. Confirm it to
   hold the tables for the period (cancelled bookings free them), then
   create its order.
3. **Table order** (walk-in): *Hotel > Restaurant > Table Order*. For a
   hotel guest, tick *Is a Hotel Guest?* and choose the folio.
4. *Generate KOT* sends the order to the kitchen; after adding items, the
   second *Generate KOT* button sends only the new items.
5. *Done* closes the order. For a hotel guest, the items are added once to
   the folio as services and invoiced with the stay.
6. Print the kitchen order ticket or the bill from the *Print* menu of the
   order, and the table reservation list and the restaurant orders per
   folio from *Hotel > Reports*.
