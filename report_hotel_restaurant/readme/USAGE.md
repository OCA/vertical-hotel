Open *Hotel > Reports > Restaurant Sales Analysis*. By default it shows the
orders that are not cancelled, with quantities and amounts by month and
food item category. Change the rows, columns and measures of the pivot, or
use the graph, to compare months, food items, customers or waiters; the
*Hotel Guests* filter keeps the orders of guests staying at the hotel.

An order with several food items has one line per item: quantities and
amounts are per item, the number of orders is counted once per order.
Kitchen order tickets are copies of the order lines and are not counted.
