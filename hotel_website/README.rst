A guest-facing hotel website for the vertical-hotel modules. Guests
browse room types, check what is free for their dates and send a
reservation request; the front desk confirms it, and a free room of the
requested type is assigned at that moment.

What guests see:

- A homepage with the hotel's name (always the company's name), a
  booking bar and the room types.
- **Rooms & Suites** (``/rooms``): every room type with photos,
  description, capacity, amenities and "from" price. With dates, each
  type shows how many rooms are still free and can be added to the stay.
- A page per room type with gallery, facts, availability and reviews.
- A stay summary, a guest details form with the accompanying guests, and
  a "request received" page.
- Their requests and reservations in the portal (``/my/reservations``).

How a website request becomes a reservation:

1. The request is saved as a **draft** reservation, marked *Booked
   Online*, with one line per requested room carrying only its room
   type, and the accompanying guests on its *Guests* tab (each one a
   contact under the guest who booked). No room is blocked yet.
2. The guest gets an acknowledgement from the reservation's chatter.
3. The receptionist presses **Confirm**: each line without a room gets a
   free room of its type (cheapest first), then the usual confirmation
   runs (overlap check, room blocking, capacity check).
4. From there it is the normal flow: confirmation email, folio,
   check-in, invoice.

Availability follows the back office rule (confirmed reservations and
folios block a room). Only rooms marked *Bookable on Website* are
offered.

Configuration
=============

- **Room type form > Website**: photo, description, display order,
  reviews.
- **Room form > Website**: include or exclude a room from online
  bookings (*Bookable on Website*).
- **Website > Configuration > Settings > Online booking times**:
  check-in and check-out times given to website requests (default 14:00
  and 11:00).
- The website menu gets *Rooms* and *Gallery*; the portal home page gets
  a *Hotel Reservations* card.
