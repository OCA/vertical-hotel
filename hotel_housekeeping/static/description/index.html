Hotel Housekeeping Management

Module for Housekeeping is the department that deals essentially with cleanliness and all ancillary service attached to that. Housekeepers are maintaining and servicing the guest rooms and replenishing stock as and when needed.

You can manage:

Housekeeping Activities: a catalogue of activities (room cleaning, making the beds, …) in hierarchical activity types.

Complete Housekeeping Process: one job per room and day, with the activities done by each housekeeper, then an inspection with a quality mark. Jobs move from Inspect to Dirty / Clean and Done, on a board grouped by status or in a calendar.

Room Housekeeping Status: each room shows whether it needs cleaning, is waiting for inspection or is ready, from its latest housekeeping job.

Activity Report: the activities of a room over a period, with the duration of each one.

Usage

  1. Configure the activities in Hotel > Configuration > Activity.
  2. Open Hotel > Housekeeping Services and create a job for a room: the date, the clean type (daily, check-in, check-out) and the inspector.
  3. Add the activities: housekeeper, start and end time.
  4. Mark the room Clean (or Dirty). The inspector sets the quality and clicks Done; the inspection time is filled in if it is empty, and must be after the end of the cleaning.
  5. The room form, list and board show the housekeeping status of the room (Needs Cleaning, To Inspect, Ready). The Housekeeping button on the room lists its jobs.
  6. Print the activities of a room from Hotel > Reports > Activity Report, or select jobs and use Print > Activity Detail.

Changelog

20.0.1.0.0

  • Migration to Odoo 20.0: access rights in security/ir.access.csv, no report_file on the report, depends on vertical_hotel only.
  • The Activity Report printed empty on Odoo 20 (t-esc); it now shows the room, the period and the activities, with the duration in hours:minutes. It can also be printed from selected jobs (Print > Activity Detail).
  • New board of housekeeping jobs grouped by status (default view), a calendar, and My Jobs, Today and Open filters.
  • New housekeeping status on rooms (Needs Cleaning, To Inspect, Ready), shown on the room form, list and board, with a Housekeeping button on the room.
  • The inspection time must now be after the end of the cleaning (the check was reversed). It is optional and filled in when the job is done.
  • The workflow is checked on the server: a job is done only after cleaning, and a done or cancelled job cannot be changed by the buttons.
  • Hotel users can remove activity lines; a loop in activity types is refused instead of freezing the server; Done works on several jobs.
  • Activity lines are deleted with their job.
