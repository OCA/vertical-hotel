Module for Housekeeping is the department that deals essentially with
cleanliness and all ancillary service attached to that. Housekeepers are
maintaining and servicing the guest rooms and replenishing stock as and
when needed.

You can manage:

**Housekeeping Activities**: a catalogue of activities (room cleaning,
making the beds, ...) in hierarchical activity types.

**Complete Housekeeping Process**: one job per room and day, with the
activities done by each housekeeper, then an inspection with a quality
mark. Jobs move from *Inspect* to *Dirty* / *Clean* and *Done*, on a board
grouped by status or in a calendar.

**Room Housekeeping Status**: each room shows whether it needs cleaning, is
waiting for inspection or is ready, from its latest housekeeping job.

**Activity Report**: the activities of a room over a period, with the
duration of each one.
